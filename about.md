{{ site.short_title }} is affiliated with the
[Center for Applied Research on Work (CAROW)]({{ site.carow.url }}) at the Cornell University
ILR School. CAROW connects research on work with the practice of putting it to use, through
training, convening, and the development of new tools.

This series is CAROW's applied-research output focused specifically on industrial relations
and employment relations — distinct from CAROW's other research hubs (AI and Work, Care
Economy, Workforce Mental Health) — though cross-cutting submissions are welcome.

Papers are circulated as working papers, not final publications: they have not necessarily
undergone peer review, and their findings and conclusions are those of the author(s), not
of CAROW or Cornell University.
