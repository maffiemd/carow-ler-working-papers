{{ site.short_title }} is co-hosted by two university research centers:

- The [Center for Applied Research on Work (CAROW)]({{ site.carow.url }}) at the
  {{ site.carow.parent }}, which connects research on work with the practice of putting it to
  use, through training, convening, and the development of new tools.
- The [Centre for Industrial Relations and Human Resources (CIRHR)]({{ site.co_host.url }}) at
  the {{ site.co_host.parent }}, a leading Canadian center for research and graduate education
  in industrial relations and human resources.

{% include co-host-banner.html %}

This series is a joint applied-research output focused specifically on industrial relations
and employment relations — distinct from CAROW's other research hubs (AI and Work, Care
Economy, Workforce Mental Health) — though cross-cutting submissions are welcome.

Papers are circulated as working papers, not final publications: they have not necessarily
undergone peer review, and their findings and conclusions are those of the author(s), not
of CAROW, CIRHR, Cornell University, or the University of Toronto.
