{{ site.short_title }} circulates new research on industrial relations, employment relations, labor-management institutions, collective bargaining, and related workplace governance topics. It is one of the applied-research outputs of the Center for Applied Research on Work (CAROW) at the Cornell University ILR School, distinct from CAROW's AI-and-Work and Care Economy hubs, though cross-cutting submissions are welcome.

Papers in this series are circulated to encourage discussion and feedback before formal peer review or journal publication. See Submit a paper for eligibility and format guidelines.
