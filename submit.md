## Scope

{{ site.short_title }} welcomes submissions on industrial relations, employment relations,
labor-management institutions, collective bargaining, labor law and policy, and related
workplace governance topics. Cross-cutting work connecting to CAROW's other research areas
(AI and work, the care economy, workforce mental health, union membership) is also welcome.

## Format

- Submit a PDF of the complete paper, including an abstract of 150–250 words.
- Include up to 3 [JEL classification codes](https://www.aeaweb.org/econlit/jelCodes.php) and
  up to 6 keywords.
- There is no fixed length limit, but papers are typically 20–50 pages.

## Review process

Submissions are read by the series editor (and, where relevant, a member of the editorial
board) for fit and basic quality. This is **not** a peer-reviewed venue — the goal is to
circulate work-in-progress for feedback ahead of formal journal submission. Typical turnaround
is _TBD_.
